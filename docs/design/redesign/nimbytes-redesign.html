Footage first, storage you can see, one place to make things

An audit of today's PWA and Mac app, and mocks of the proposed redesign. Built on the current Mist and Midnight tokens. Nothing here is built yet: pick what to build after review.

Proposal · waiting for owner review 6 phone screens 4 Mac screens 25 audit findings 3 token changes

Five big moves

Each mock below applies one or more of these. They are independent, so they can ship one at a time.

1. A Studio tab

"Shorts" becomes "Studio" with a Shorts | Productions switch. Phase 14 gets a home without a sixth tab or a later nav rework.

2. Storage as three plain states

In the cloud, on this Mac for a few minutes, kept offline. One icon each, on every tile, plus a meter that visibly drops back to 0.

3. Three steps, not nine words

Uploading, Understanding, Ready. Engine words like "Making proxy" move to the clip's detail line.

4. Search that says what you can do

A Select button, labelled checkboxes and a bar that always reads "2 moments · 0:15 · Make a short".

5. One map for phone and Mac

Same sections, same words, same order. Settings get one home (⌘, on Mac). Upload stops appearing twice.
