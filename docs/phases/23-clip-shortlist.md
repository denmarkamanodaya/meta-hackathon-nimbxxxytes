# Phase 23 clip shortlist: test footage for Ready, Respond, Recover

Research date: 2026-10-28. Read-only web research. No media was downloaded.

Every row below is a page that was opened. The footage itself was not viewed. "What it shows" comes from the page title, description and tags. Preview every clip before use, especially for faces and licence plates. No page mentions licence plates.

## DVIDS licence line (applies to the A rows unless the row says otherwise)

Each DVIDS page shows the label **"PUBLIC DOMAIN"** and this sentence:

> "This work, [title], by [name], identified by DVIDS, must comply with the restrictions shown on https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright."

In the tables, "PD + std line" means the page showed both the label and this sentence. When the fetch returned only part of the text, the row says so.

No DVIDS page states the source resolution. The sizes 800x450 to 1920x1080 on each page are only embed options, so the resolution column is left out of table A.

## A1. Hurricane Helene, Asheville and Swannanoa, Buncombe County, North Carolina, USA

- **Dates:** 5 Oct 2024 to 1 Feb 2025. The two outside rows (Burnsville and Mitchell County) run to 8 Apr 2025.
- **Crews:** USACE Pittsburgh District (Michel Sauret), USACE Wilmington District (Charles Delano, Stacey Reese, Michael Davis), FEMA (Matthew Keegan) and the 412th Theater Engineer Command.

**Overlap notes:**
- **Asheville debris, 5 Oct 2024:** 939219 (B-roll) and 939288 (edited reel) have the same camera operator (Delano), the same day and the same debris teams. They are the strongest same-place pair.
- **Asheville, other dates:** 940064 (11 Oct, a different crew) and 941492 (23–24 Oct, NC Arboretum roads) are in the same city, but the exact streets are not confirmed.
- **Swannanoa:** 943308 (1 Nov), 940625 (16 Oct), 939972 (11 Oct) and 951167 (1 Feb 2025, Azalea Park, by the river) are all in the same small town. The raw 14:48 master (943308) is the most likely to overlap with the others.

## Caveats summary

1. **DVIDS licence:** every page says "PUBLIC DOMAIN" and points to https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright. That page sets these conditions:
   - **Endorsement:** no implied endorsement. Show the disclaimer "The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement".
   - **People:** "VI postings by the DoW do not waive any publicity or privacy rights of any individuals portrayed".
   - **Insignia:** commercial users should obscure unit insignia and Service names.
   - **Third-party material:** some content may still carry rights owned by others.
2. **Pexels and Pixabay licences:**
   - **Pexels:** "Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light". Do not sell unaltered copies, and do not imply endorsement.
   - **Pixabay:** no standalone redistribution. Limits apply to recognisable people and to trademarks.
   - **AI training:** neither licence page mentions it.
3. **Items to avoid or treat with care:**
   - **938842:** strip the audio (CapCut music, rights not owned).
   - **Edited or interview-led items:** 939288, 939692, 940625, 885954, 886529 and 887313.
   - **Weak fits:** 884751, Pexels 10852314 and Pixabay 26566.
   - **People and plates:** treat every Pexels/Pixabay clip with people as having recognisable faces, and check the flood clips for licence plates.
4. **DVIDS account:** an older DVIDS-hosted notice says HD downloads need a free DVIDS account (register, log in, then "Download Video"). This was not confirmed on the current site, and the current copyright page does not mention an account.
5. **Formats and sizes:**
   - **DVIDS:** no page states the source resolution or file size. Most items run 0:13–3:00. 943308 (14:48) and 958263 (7:02) are long raw masters.
   - **Pexels/Pixabay:** MP4 (Pixabay states "MP4"). Clips run 7–49 seconds, from 1080p to 4K. Five are vertical 9:16, which matters when mixing orientations in the fusion test.
