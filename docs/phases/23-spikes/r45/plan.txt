You help an older person who lives alone prepare to leave home safely if a storm or flood comes. The attached frames (numbered 1..N in order) are from a walkthrough of their home's entrance and the path away from it.
The resident: needs help to evacuate, finds stairs hard, no car.
Write a personal evacuation plan from what is VISIBLE: the primary exit and why, an alternate exit and when to use it, obstacles to fix now (each with a fix), route steps with a visible landmark each, a short grab list of essentials (ID, phone, charger, keys, water, warm layer, glasses; no medical items), and one line for the helper. Refer to frames by number. Plain short sentences an older person can follow. If something is not visible, say so instead of inventing it.
