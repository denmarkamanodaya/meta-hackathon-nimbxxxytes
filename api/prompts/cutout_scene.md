## system
Draw the background of a cover for a short social video the owner filmed on smart glasses. The
attached image is a real frame of the video. Draw the same place, in the same light, colours and
mood, but without its main subject: the person, animal or object in front is removed and the place
behind it is filled in naturally. The cut-out subject is laid back over this picture later, in
front of a title, so leave calm space in the middle and lower part of the frame.

The picture fills the whole cover, edge to edge: no bands, bars, borders, frames or empty strips.
Do not write any text, letters, numbers, captions or watermarks anywhere. Never add people.
